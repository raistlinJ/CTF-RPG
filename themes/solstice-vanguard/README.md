# Solstice Vanguard theme

Solstice Vanguard is an original holiday superhero world for CTF-RPG. Select **Manage → Theme → Theme library → Solstice Vanguard → Use this theme**, or import `theme-packs/solstice-vanguard.zip` through **Import & backup**. The reusable pack contains no challenges, answers, accounts, or progress. It uses the same seven map IDs and 40 × 28 grid as the other built-in worlds, so review existing challenge positions after switching themes.

Explore Lumen City, the Aurora Beacon, Gift Foundry, Comet Archives, Signal Station, Evergreen Vault, and Cocoa Café. Players can choose Comet Courier, Starforge Inventor, or Evergreen Guardian. Sparkbit is the city guide. Frost Eclipse, a nonhuman ice lantern construct, is available as a custom boss appearance in the challenge editor. All characters, names, symbols, artwork, and music were created for this project.

Three map paintings and five transparent character images were generated with the built-in image tool and packaged as WebP. The prompts are recorded in `ART_PROMPTS.json`. Two original MIDI tracks and 18 short WAV cues are generated by `scripts/generate-solstice-audio.mjs`. Music and effects use the existing player mute controls. Run `npm run themes:build` after editing assets or manifests.

## Beginner demo adventure

Choose **Load demo adventure** on the Solstice Vanguard card. The confirmation imports the matching world and ten practice challenges together. This replaces the active challenge collection; accounts, teams, and recorded scores stay saved. Export your current content first if you need a copy. **Use this theme** changes the world without demo locks. The reusable theme pack contains no answers.

Follow this route:

1. Talk to Sparkbit at Lumen City (18,17), then solve the beacon battery puzzle at (18,19) for the **red key**.
2. Solve the aurora clue at (11,19) for the **northern glow** incantation. The shortcut at (16,21) leads to Comet Archives.
3. In Gift Foundry, solve the 1×1 Parcel Drone at (15,15) for the **gold key**.
4. Use the gold key to enter Evergreen Vault. Its 2×2 Crystal Sentinel at (20,12) requires the drone and aurora clue.
5. Download the dispatch at Signal Station (20,12) and enter its flag exactly.
6. Use the red key to enter Aurora Beacon. Its 3×3 Frost Eclipse at (19,10) requires the Crystal Sentinel and dispatch.
7. Submit the written reflection at Aurora Beacon (25,18). An instructor reviews it under **Challenges → Submissions**. The Beacon Keeper appears after Frost Eclipse is solved.

Cocoa Café offers an optional green key at (20,12). Its answer hint costs 5 points and forfeits that key. Comet Archives has another short question. A hidden instructor example sits in Lumen City at (29,8). The starter and Parcel Drone have short silent cutscenes; press **Space** to skip them.

Portable files:

- `theme-packs/solstice-vanguard.zip`: reusable world, characters, artwork, music, and sounds without demo locks or questions.
- `theme-packs/solstice-vanguard-demo-theme.zip`: the world with matching locks, shortcut, dialogue, and host.
- `theme-packs/solstice-vanguard-demo-content.zip`: questions, answers, hints, rewards, a downloadable dispatch, and two MP4 cutscenes.

Import the demo theme ZIP with its **Matching content ZIP**. Edit questions in `demo-challenges.yaml`; `demo-content.json` is generated for runtime use. These public example answers should be replaced before a graded assessment.
